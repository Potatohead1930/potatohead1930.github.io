About these values

Values. Codes tagged 2026 use the published 2026 Medicare work RVU. Codes tagged est are the pre-2026 value reduced 2.5% for the 2026 efficiency adjustment, so check those against your fee schedule. E/M codes were not cut.

Multiple procedures. With the reduction on, each patient's highest-valued procedure counts at 100% and other procedures at 50%. Add-on codes, radiology interpretations and E/M visits are never reduced.

Bilateral. Modifier -50 credits 150% of the single-side value.

Clinic days. Visit counts are separate patients, so no multiple-procedure reduction applies. G2211 is counted only as entered. Office procedures billed with a visit need modifier -25 on the visit.

Codes not in the list. Type the 5-digit code and press Return, then enter its wRVU.
