Privacy Policy – Inbox Lens

Last updated: September 2026

Who this app is for. Inbox Lens is a personal app for the owner's own use only. It is not offered to the public and no one else uses it.

What access it requests. The app requests read-only access to the owner's Gmail account (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete, or modify email.

How data is handled. Email data is processed locally on the owner's own device. It is not uploaded to any server operated by the app.

No sharing. Data is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party.

No tracking. This website and the app use no analytics, cookies, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Google API Services User Data Policy. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes. This policy may be updated by revising this page and its date.
