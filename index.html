Inbox Lens

Inbox Lens is a personal application built for the private use of its owner. It reads the owner's own email, in read-only mode, to display information for the owner's personal convenience.

The app is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not collect information from anyone else.

Requested Google access: read-only access to the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly). The app cannot send, delete, or modify email.
